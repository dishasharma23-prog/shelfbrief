01 Polite scraping

It asks before
it reads.

Every request is checked against the site's robots.txt first. Requests to one store are at least 1.5 seconds apart. When a store answers 429 Too Many Requests, the crawler waits as long as the store asks and doesn't push.

  • Reads robots.txt first

    An RFC 9309 parser with wildcards and $ anchors. Python's built-in parser ignores wildcards, and Shopify's default robots.txt is full of them.

  • Backs off when asked

    Retries only on 429 and 5xx, with exponential backoff, honouring Retry-After. A 404 is never retried.

  • Says who it is

    Each request carries ShelfBriefBot/1.0 and a link to the source, so a site owner can see what it is.

  • Public product data only

    Catalog JSON and product pages. No logins, no checkout, no customer reviews or personal data.

    04 Campaign briefs

    Briefs a creator
    can shoot tomorrow.

    For each brand, the best-reviewed, newest and most claim-rich products get a brief: who it's for, which creators fit, three opening hooks and the formats to shoot. Proof points are only claims printed on the brand's own page, with ASCI disclosure rules built in.
